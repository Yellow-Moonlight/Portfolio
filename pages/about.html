Hi! I’m Seongjoo Moon, a media artist exploring the relationship between humans and technology, crossing the boundaries between art and engineering. I work with all sorts of technical media—web programming, physical computing, sound and video art, 3D modeling, and digital fabrication.

Lately, I’ve been focusing on developing toy instruments for children. I’m interested in finding new ways to mix play, learning, and technology, and to create artistic experiences that are fun, interactive, and full of curiosity.
