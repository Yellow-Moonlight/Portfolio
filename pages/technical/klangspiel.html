KlangSpiel

2026

Microcontroller, Arcade Joystick and Button, wood, 4:3 Monitor, Raspberry Pi, Umfeld

<The Small Computing Group>, Bremen(Germany), 2026

KlangSpiel is a two-player sound-game console and an evolving creative platform. Designed with a physical structure reminiscent of a cocktail arcade machine, it invites two players to sit face-to-face, transforming the competitive nature of traditional gaming into a collaborative act of musical composition.
