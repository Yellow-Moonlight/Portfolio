Inherited Injustice

An installation and performance that confront how we continue to live upon—and consume—the sacrifices of those who came before us.

2019

A silicone mold was cast using a 3D printed model, and this silicone mold was used to create a usable charcoal sculpture with a custom-developed mixture of charcoal and agyo (a traditional Korean adhesive).
