Generous Gerry - Ijae

2025

Microcontroller, Processing

At the request of Ijae, an interactive lighting system was designed for her performance. A total of four light bulbs were used, and the lights were programmed to react to sound. To accurately measure the sound values, Processing was used to receive the audio, and this sound data was sent to a microcontroller (Arduino Nano) to control the light bulbs using MOSFETs.
