3 Key Macro Keyboard

2026

PCB Design

This is a macro keyboard designed as a welcome kit for new Digital Media students at HfK for the 2026/27 winter semester. Because multiple keyboards had to be produced within a limited budget, the CH552E microcontroller was used to keep the unit price under 2 euros. The prototype was confirmed to work as planned, and the final product has been ordered. I am currently waiting for the PCBs to arrive.
