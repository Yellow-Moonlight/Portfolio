Apple

Apple is a tactile sound object that explores the physics of movement. By tilting a minimalist wooden box, users navigate a digital apple across an OLED screen, triggering diverse synthesized sounds as it strikes the boundaries.

2026

teensy, accelerometer, oled, knobs

Apple is an interactive installation that translates physical orientation into a playful sonic experience. Housed in a compact enclosure, the work features a digital apple trapped within the confines of an OLED display. Utilizing an internal accelerometer, the apple reacts to the user’s touch and tilt, sliding and bouncing against virtual walls to generate rhythmic pulses. A series of analog knobs allows for real-time manipulation of the sound—from sharp, percussive 'bites' to lingering ambient echoes. By turning a simple fruit into a musical interface, the project invites users to rediscover the joy of tactile play and sound synthesis.
