Time for Training

Time for Training is an audiovisual work structured around an original musical composition. The project utilizes a 1:1 mapping system where each individual audio stem corresponds to a specific visual layer, creating a multi-layered digital composition. The opening sequence features a recurring motif of a ping-pong ball failing to reach its destination, presenting a loop of repetitive and incomplete physical actions. The work focuses on the structural and temporal synchronization between audio components and video layers, allowing the audience to freely interpret the underlying narrative.

2020
