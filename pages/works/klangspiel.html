KlangSpiel

KlangSpiel is a dual-player sound-game console and a generative platform for interactive audio-visual experiences.

2026-

Joystick, Wood, 4:3 Monitor, Raspberry Pi, Umfeld

KlangSpiel is a two-player sound-game console and an evolving creative platform. Designed with a physical structure reminiscent of a cocktail arcade machine, it invites two players to sit face-to-face, transforming the competitive nature of traditional gaming into a collaborative act of musical composition.

The console hosts a series of original games developed by the artist, utilizing the Umfeld framework by Dennis P. Paul to bridge interactive play with complex sound synthesis. Within this environment, players do not merely play for a high score; they become co-composers. Every mechanical input—from button presses to joystick movements—triggers rhythmic structures and sonic textures in real-time, making each session a unique, ephemeral performance.

Rather than a standalone device, KlangSpiel is conceived as an expansive platform. It serves as a dedicated ecosystem for a growing library of "playable music" titles, where I will continue to develop new interactive experiences. By integrating the nostalgia of arcade culture with contemporary algorithmic sound, KlangSpiel explores the synergy between intuitive play and the structured beauty of musical harmony.
